## Introduction to Mathematics, Data Science and AI

I serve as an instructor for **"Introduction to Mathematics, Data Science and AI"** (数理・データサイエンス・AI入門), a university-wide general education course (Innovation Education subject) at <a href="https://www.ynu.ac.jp/" target="_blank">Yokohama National University</a>. The course is a core component of the <a href="https://www.itsc.ynu.ac.jp/y-mda/index.html" target="_blank">YOKOHAMA MDA Program</a> (a government-accredited Mathematics, Data Science and AI education program at the literacy level) and is open to students across all faculties.

### Objectives

The course helps students acquire the foundational literacy needed to make use of mathematics, data science, and AI in everyday life and work. It emphasizes understanding the data around us, reading and communicating data, and explaining findings to others, combining on-demand flipped-learning materials with group work to encourage active learning.

### Syllabus (15 sessions)

1. Changes happening in society
2. Data used in society
3. Application areas of data and AI
4. Data and AI in practice
5. Data science and privacy
6. Responsibility in data science
7. AI in society
8. Social surveys and data
9. Problem solving with data
10. Reading data 1
11. Reading data 2
12. Reading data 3
13. Explaining data 1
14. Explaining data 2
15. Data literacy and data utilization
